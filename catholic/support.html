Add a widget to your Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the area below the clock → choose Catholic Widgets and a widget.

Add a widget to your Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → search for Catholic Widgets → pick a size → Add Widget.

A widget looks out of date

Widgets refresh on a schedule iOS controls. Opening the app refreshes them right away. If one still looks wrong, remove it and add it again.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Catholic Widgets Pro → Cancel Subscription. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
