Privacy Policy

Christian Widgets · Last updated November 3, 2026

Christian Widgets does not collect any data about you.

What the app stores

Your settings (looks, fonts, widget options), saved verses and progress are kept only on your iPhone, shared between the app and its widgets. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, contains no analytics or third-party code, and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not connect to the internet; all text is built into the app.

Purchases

Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Pro is active.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
